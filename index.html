    Battle ship:

    Battleship is a strategy game where your goal is to sink all of the computer’s hidden ships before it sinks yours. At the start of the game, you place your fleet on your board by choosing a ship, selecting a starting square, and picking a direction (up, down, left, or right). Once all ships are placed, the battle begins. Click on squares on the computer’s grid to fire your shots—if you hit a ship, it will be marked, and if you miss, the square will show as a miss. After each of your turns, the AI will take its turn, using its own logic to search for and target your ships. Pay attention to your hits, as ships occupy multiple spaces in a straight line, and use that information to finish them off. The first player to successfully hit all 17 ship segments wins the game.
